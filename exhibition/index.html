A World of Collages

About the Exhibition

For my exhibition, I decided to choose three different net arts that showed a combination of different things. I feel like they use similar techniques to show a collection of different ideas in different ways. I would say that the theme of all 3 are different, but the idea is the same which is too show off different combinations of net work in a collage. I would say that the different authors of all three had different ideas and ways they wanted there art to be persevered, but at the end of the day, the idea of a collage shows different ideas together.
